Jezero Crater's ancient lake-delta system offers many promising sampling targets. Parts of Jezero may be especially rich in carbonates, minerals that, on Earth, can preserve fossilized signs of ancient life and can be associated with biological processes. And new landing technologies will allow Perseverance to touch down even closer to the most promising locations than any Mars mission before it.
